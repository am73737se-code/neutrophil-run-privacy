Neutrophil Run

Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how information is handled when you play Neutrophil Run.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Your privacy matters. Neutrophil Run is an independent educational mobile game developed by Mohammad Seyed Amiri. This policy describes the game's current information practices.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Neutrophil Run mobile game. It explains what information the game handles and how that information is used.

2. Information We Collect

Neutrophil Run does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play. The game does not independently collect personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

This statement concerns information collected by the game developer. The app store or platform through which you download the game may process information under its own privacy policy.

3. Game Data and Local Storage

The game may save gameplay information—such as progress, scores, settings, or selected characters—locally on your device so that game features work properly.

The game does not independently send this locally stored gameplay information to servers operated by the developer.

4. Advertisements and Analytics

The current version of Neutrophil Run does not display third-party advertisements and does not use third-party analytics services to track players' activity or gameplay behavior.

5. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are not currently available. Future versions may offer optional purchases, such as additional playable characters, through supported app-store payment services. Cafe Bazaar and Poolakey are being considered for this purpose.

If payment services are added, the relevant payment provider will process the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not intend to receive or store your full payment-card details. This policy will be updated to describe the services actually integrated before those features are released.

6. Children's Privacy

Neutrophil Run is an educational game intended to be accessible to players of different ages. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

Gameplay data stored locally is subject to the security and settings of your device. We recommend keeping your device's operating system and security protections up to date.

8. Third-Party Services

The current version does not include advertising or analytics services. If third-party services are introduced in a future version—for example, to support in-app purchases—this policy will be revised to identify the relevant services and explain their role.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features or information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. Please check the “Last updated” date at the top.
